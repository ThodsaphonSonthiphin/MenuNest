Behaviour guards — part of the decision, not implementation detail:
  • The rail never hides while the dial is open.
  • It returns after roughly 900ms of idle, so it cannot be lost by stopping mid-flick.
  • It only starts hiding past ~40px of scroll, with an ~8px jitter threshold, so a nudge does not flicker it.
Syncfusion SpeedDial has no hide-on-scroll of its own. That behaviour, and the three guards above, are roughly twenty lines MenuNest owns on top of the component. Budget for them; they are the part no library ships.
Change history is a new screen. This mock shows its button, not its content — what it lists and how far back it reaches is the open change-history-view ticket, which is blocked behind history-storage.
